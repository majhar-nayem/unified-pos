# ADR-020: Surcharging is data, not a Phase 1 feature; least-cost-routing visibility is a payments reporting requirement

- Status: **Accepted 18 Oct 2026**
- Decision area: surcharges and card cost
- Related: [01 §7.4 (`capabilities.surcharging`), §14](../01-system-architecture.md), [05 G3, §6.2 item 4](../05-competitive-review-and-plan-critique.md), [03 §6 M12](../03-implementation-plan.md), [payments/capability-matrix.md §5](../payments/capability-matrix.md), [ADR-010](ADR-010.md)

## Context

The RBA's conclusions on its review of merchant card payment costs and surcharging end surcharging on eftpos, Mastercard and Visa cards from 1 October 2026, with lower domestic interchange caps from the same date; least-cost routing expectations are unchanged (RBA conclusions paper, March 2026, as reported by CommBank, HSF Kramer, JD Supra; see capability matrix §5 for sources and the unresolved question of American Express). Tyro's own documentation says its integrated surcharge feature is deprecated and retired by October 2026, with surcharge rates set to zero and new integrations advised to send `enableSurcharge: false`. The date has passed (today is 2026-10-18), so building a surcharge UI for Australian domestic cards would be wasted scope and a compliance risk. What merchants will compare instead is processing cost.

## Decision

- Keep `surcharge_minor` columns in the schema (pricing charges, payment details) for non-AU markets and for card brands outside the ban; they are data, not a feature.
- **No surcharge UI for Australia** in Phase 1 or 2. Adapters send surcharge-disabled flags (Tyro `enableSurcharge: false`) and the adapter capability `surcharging` is `false` for AU accounts by default; any non-zero provider-returned surcharge is stored and shown on receipts (it may arise from an account not yet switched off) and raises an alert.
- Add `leastCostRouting` to adapter capabilities, record the network a payment was processed on, and ship **least-cost-routing visibility** (network mix, effective rate by network) in M12's payment cost report.
- Do not claim card-fee recovery features anywhere in product copy for AU.

## Alternatives considered

1. **Build the surcharge UI as planned.** Rejected: banned for the main schemes from 1 October 2026.
2. **Remove the columns.** Rejected: other markets (NZ, others) and excluded brands may still surcharge; removal is a migration for no benefit.

## Consequences

- Positive: scope removed from F5/F8; a differentiating cost report replaces it; fewer legal exposures.
- Negative: the scope of the ban for American Express and other non-designated schemes is reported inconsistently by secondary sources; legal review before any AU surcharge capability is reintroduced.
- Receipts and refunds must still carry any surcharge amount on the original sale (Tyro: refund amount includes the original surcharge) for sales made before the ban.
