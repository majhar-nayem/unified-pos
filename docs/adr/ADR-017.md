# ADR-017: Postgres read models first; ClickHouse in Phase 3

- Status: **Accepted** (plan §13 open decision 7: adopt ClickHouse in Phase 3 or defer to Phase 4 depending on reporting demand)
- Decision area: reporting and analytics
- Related: [01 §10](../01-system-architecture.md), [03 §5 F9, §7 item 11](../03-implementation-plan.md), [ADR-006](ADR-006.md), [ADR-013](ADR-013.md)

## Context

Phase 1–2 reporting is operational: today's sales, X/Z reports, sales by item and payment method, cash variance. Data volumes are modest and the correctness requirement (totals equal ledger sums) is higher than the need for ad-hoc scale. A warehouse from day one adds a pipeline and a second source of truth.

## Decision

Maintain Postgres read models (`rm_sales_daily`, `rm_item_sales_daily`, `rm_payment_method_daily`, `rm_cash_variance_daily`, `rm_kitchen_times_daily`) via event consumers, with live counters in Redis for "today". Historical and financial reports use the same read models plus queries on a read replica and asynchronous exports to S3. A nightly reconciliation job proves read-model totals against `orders` and `payment_intents` per business date and alerts on drift. In Phase 3 add ClickHouse fed from outbox events (Debezium CDC as fallback) with dbt models and BI connectors for basket analysis, turnover and benchmarking. Every report states time zone, currency and accounting convention.

## Alternatives considered

1. **Warehouse from day one.** Rejected: cost and a second source of truth before demand.
2. **Query the OLTP tables directly for everything.** Rejected: contention with checkout and unbounded query cost.

## Consequences

- Positive: simple stack; freshness visible per report; proof jobs keep read models honest.
- Negative: read-model lag during incidents (degradation matrix, architecture §12.2); heavy ad-hoc analytics wait for Phase 3.
- Security: read models are a second place where tenant data lives; they carry `tenant_id` and RLS like any table, and exports to S3 are tenant-scoped, signed-URL, short-lived ([threat model](../security/threat-model.md), information disclosure).
